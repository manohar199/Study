#### 1. Official APPSC Physics Syllabus Hierarchy

- **Topic 1: Units and Dimensions**
  - **Subtopic 1.1:** Introduction, Physical quantity
  - **Subtopic 1.2:** Fundamental and Derived quantities, Fundamental and Derived units
  - **Subtopic 1.3:** SI units, Multiples and Sub-multiples, Rules for writing S.I. units, Advantages of SI units
  - **Subtopic 1.4:** Dimensions and Dimensional formulae, Dimensional constants and Dimensionless quantities
  - **Subtopic 1.5:** Principle of homogeneity, Advantages and limitations of dimensional analysis
  - **Subtopic 1.6:** Errors in measurement (Absolute error, Relative error, Percentage error), Significant figures, Problems

- **Topic 2: Elements of Vectors**
  - **Subtopic 2.1:** Scalars and Vectors, Types of vectors (Proper, Null, Unit, Equal, Negative, Like, Co-Initial, Co-planar, Position)
  - **Subtopic 2.2:** Addition of vectors, Representation of vectors, Resolution of vectors
  - **Subtopic 2.3:** Vector Addition Laws: Parallelogram law, Triangle law, Polygon law; Subtraction of vectors
  - **Subtopic 2.4:** Dot and Cross products of vectors, Problems

- **Topic 3: Dynamics**
  - **Subtopic 3.1:** Acceleration due to gravity, Equations of motion for a freely falling body and body thrown vertically up
  - **Subtopic 3.2:** Projectiles: Horizontal and Oblique projections (Maximum height, Time of flight, Range)
  - **Subtopic 3.3:** Definitions & Concepts: Force, Momentum, Angular displacement, Angular velocity, Angular acceleration, Angular momentum, Moment of Inertia, Torque, Problems

- **Topic 4: Friction**
  - **Subtopic 4.1:** Introduction, Causes, Types of friction
  - **Subtopic 4.2:** Laws of friction, Angle of repose, Angle of friction
  - **Subtopic 4.3:** Motion on a rough inclined plane, Advantages & Disadvantages, Methods of reducing friction, Problems

- **Topic 5: Work, Power and Energy**
  - **Subtopic 5.1:** Work, Power, Energy definitions & explanations
  - **Subtopic 5.2:** Potential energy & Kinetic energy derivations; K.E. and Momentum relation
  - **Subtopic 5.3:** Work-Energy Theorem, Law of Conservation of Energy, Problems

- **Topic 6: Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)**
  - **Subtopic 6.1:** Introduction, Conditions of SHM, Definition, Examples
  - **Subtopic 6.2:** Expressions for Displacement, Velocity, Acceleration, Time Period, Frequency, Phase
  - **Subtopic 6.3:** Simple Pendulum: Time period expression, Laws of simple pendulum, Seconds pendulum, Problems

- **Topic 7: Heat and Thermodynamics**
  - **Subtopic 7.1:** Expansion of Gases, Boyle’s Law, Absolute scale of temperature, Charles’s Laws
  - **Subtopic 7.2:** Ideal Gas Equation, Universal Gas Constant (\(R\)), Difference between Gas Constant (\(r\)) and Universal Gas Constant (\(R\))
  - **Subtopic 7.3:** Isothermal and Adiabatic processes, Laws of Thermodynamics
  - **Subtopic 7.4:** Specific heats, Molar specific heats of a gas (\(C_p, C_v\))
  - **Subtopic 7.5:** Modes of Heat Transmission, Laws of thermal conductivity, Coefficient of thermal conductivity, Problems

- **Topic 8: Sound**
  - **Subtopic 8.1:** Nature of Sound, Types of wave motion, Musical sound vs Noise
  - **Subtopic 8.2:** Noise Pollution: Causes, Effects, Methods of reduction
  - **Subtopic 8.3:** Beats, Doppler Effect, Echo
  - **Subtopic 8.4:** Acoustics: Reverberation, Reverberation time, Sabine’s formula, Conditions of a good auditorium, Problems

- **Topic 9: Properties of Matter**
  - **Subtopic 9.1:** Elasticity: Stress, Strain, Units & Dimensions, Hooke’s Law
  - **Subtopic 9.2:** Modulii of Elasticity: Young’s (\(Y\)), Bulk (\(K\)), Rigidity (\(\eta\)), Poisson’s ratio (\(\sigma\)), Interrelations (equations only)
  - **Subtopic 9.3:** Surface Tension: Molecular theory, Angle of contact, Capillarity & formula, Examples
  - **Subtopic 9.4:** Viscosity: Newton’s law of viscous force, Coefficient of viscosity (\(\eta\)), Effect of temperature on viscosity, Poiseuille’s equation, Problems

- **Topic 10: Electricity & Magnetism**
  - **Subtopic 10.1:** Ohm’s Law, Specific Resistance, Kirchhoff’s Laws, Wheatstone Bridge, Meter Bridge
  - **Subtopic 10.2:** Magnetism: Coulomb’s Inverse Square Law, Magnetic field, Lines of force
  - **Subtopic 10.3:** Magnetic Induction Field Strength (\(B\)): Field on Axial line and Equatorial line of a bar magnet, Problems

- **Topic 11: Modern Physics**
  - **Subtopic 11.1:** Photoelectric Effect: Einstein’s equation, Laws, Photoelectric cell & applications
  - **Subtopic 11.2:** Fiber Optics: Total Internal Reflection, Working principle, Types, Applications
  - **Subtopic 11.3:** Superconductivity: Principles & Applications
  - **Subtopic 11.4:** Nanotechnology: Definition, Nanomaterials, Applications

#### 3. Source Gap Analysis & Additional Material Required

While H.C. Verma Vol 1 and Vol 2 cover **~92% of the APPSC IPO Physics Syllabus**, the following specific syllabus items are **partially covered or not covered in H.C. Verma** and require supplementary material.

1. **Topic 1 (Units & Dimensions):** Specific S.I. formatting/grammatical rules (e.g., capitalization, pluralization, punctuation) and explicit lists of advantages of SI units.
2. **Topic 7 (Heat & Thermodynamics):** Explicit distinction between specific gas constant (\(r\)) and universal gas constant (\(R\)).
3. **Topic 8 (Sound):** Noise Pollution causes/methods of abatement, detailed Sabine’s Formula for reverberation time (\(T = \frac{0.161V}{A}\)), and explicit conditions for a good auditorium.
4. **Topic 11 (Modern Physics):** Fiber optics classification (Step-index vs. Graded-index, Single-mode vs. Multi-mode), Superconductivity (Meissner effect, Type I/Type II, BCS theory overview), and Nanotechnology basics.
