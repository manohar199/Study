## PART 1: MASTER APPSC IPO SYLLABUS BREAKDOWN & NCERT 9–12 MAPPING

### 1. Official Syllabus Structure

The official APPSC IPO Technical Syllabus for Physics and Chemistry is structured as follows:

- **Physics (11 Core Units):**

  1. Units and Dimensions
  2. Elements of Vectors
  3. Dynamics
  4. Friction
  5. Work, Power and Energy
  6. Simple Harmonic Motion
  7. Heat and Thermodynamics
  8. Sound
  9. Properties of Matter
  10. Electricity and Magnetism
  11. Modern Physics

- **Chemistry (11 Core Units):**
  1. Fundamentals of Chemistry (Atomic Structure & Chemical Bonding)
  2. Solutions
  3. Acids and Bases
  4. Principles of Metallurgy
  5. Electrochemistry
  6. Corrosion
  7. Water Technology
  8. Polymers
  9. Fuels
  10. Chemistry in Daily Life
  11. Environmental Studies

### 3. NCERT Study Guidance: Deep Study vs. Read Once vs. Skip

- **Deep Study (High Yield for APPSC IPO):**
  - _Physics:_ Class 11 Ch 2 (Units & Measurements), Ch 3 & 4 (Kinematics), Ch 5 (Laws of Motion), Ch 6 (Work, Energy, Power), Ch 10 (Fluids), Class 12 Ch 3 (Current Electricity), Ch 11 (Dual Nature), etc.
  - _Chemistry:_ Class 11 Ch 1 (Mole Concept), Ch 2 (Atomic Structure), Ch 4 (Chemical Bonding), Class 12 Ch 3 (Electrochemistry), Ch 6 (Metallurgy), Ch 15 (Polymers).
- **Read Once:**
  - _Physics:_ Class 11 Ch 1 (Physical World), Class 12 Ch 15 (Communication Systems).
  - _Chemistry:_ Class 12 Ch 16 (Chemistry in Everyday Life), Class 11 Ch 14 (Environmental Chemistry).
- **Skip (Irrelevant for APPSC IPO Syllabus):**
  - Complex theoretical derivations in Quantum Mechanics/Advanced Wave Optics not mentioned in APPSC syllabus.
  - Biological taxonomy/Tissues portions of Class 9 Science (focus strictly on Physics/Chemistry chapters).
